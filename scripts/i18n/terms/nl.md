| switchableSocket | switchable socket | Socket, plug or relay that is switched on and off, integrated as a charger. | schakelbaar stopcontact |
| tariff | tariff | Price structure over time for grid import or grid export, static or dynamic. | tarief |
| tariffSurcharge | surcharge | Fixed amount per kWh added to the energy price, e.g. grid fees or provider margin. Not the charge level. | opslag |
| usage | usage | Energy overview view showing where energy went. | gebruik |

| vehicle | vehicle | An EV known to evcc, with its battery capacity and charge level. | voertuig |
| vehicleLimit | vehicle limit | Charge limit set in the vehicle itself, not in evcc. | voertuiglimiet |
| vehicleRange | range | Distance a vehicle can drive, in km or mi. | actieradius |
| withdrawal | withdrawal | Energy taken from a costly source: grid import and battery discharge. Counterpart of production in the energy chart, which groups energy used directly from own sources. Not a financial term. | afname |
