## Glossary

Agreed term per concept. Base form; inflect as the grammar requires.

| key | en | description | de |

| consumer | consumer | A metered load that evcc does not control, part of house consumption. | Verbraucher |

| dialog | dialog | Overlay opened from a page for settings or confirmations. Called modal only in code. | Dialog |
| dynamicTariff | dynamic tariff | Tariff whose prices change over time, e.g. every hour or 15 minutes. | dynamischer Tarif |
| emissions | emissions | CO₂ caused by charged energy, in kg or g/kWh. | Emissionen |
| energy | energy | Amount over time, in Wh or kWh. Not power. | Energie |
| energyFlow | energy flow | Live diagram of production, grid, battery and consumption. | Energiefluss |
| energyPrice | energy price | Price per kWh at a point in time. | Energiepreis |
| evcc | evcc | Product name. Always lowercase in every language, also at sentence start. | evcc |
| externalLimit | external limit | Limit imposed by the grid operator or an external system. Code says HEMS for historical reasons; never use that acronym for this feature in UI text. | externe Begrenzung |
| forecast | forecast | Solar, price or CO₂ forecast. | Vorhersage |
| grid | grid | Public electricity grid. | Netz |
| gridExport | grid export | Energy fed into the grid. Short label export inside a grid card. One term throughout, no synonyms such as feed-in. | Einspeisung |
| gridExportTariff | grid export tariff | Tariff for energy fed into the grid, i.e. the remuneration. Builds on the grid export term; one word for both is fine if the language has no distinction. | Einspeisevergütung |
| gridImport | grid import | Energy taken from the grid. Short label import inside a grid card. | Netzbezug |
| gridImportTariff | grid import tariff | Tariff for energy taken from the grid. | Netzbezugstarif |
| gridMeter | grid meter | Meter at the grid connection point. | Netzzähler |
| guestVehicle | guest vehicle | Unknown vehicle at a charging point, not configured in evcc. | Gastfahrzeug |
| heatingDevice | heating device | Device level: heat pump, heating rod etc. The heating counterpart of a charger. | Heizgerät |
| homeBattery | home battery | Stationary battery in the house, as opposed to the vehicle battery. | Hausbatterie |
| loadpoint | charging point or heater | Developer term for the controllable unit on top of a device, i.e. a charging point or a heater. Never used in UI text. | Ladepunkt oder Heizung |
| loadpointCharging | charging point | Loadpoint built on a charger, controls a vehicle. | Ladepunkt |
| loadpointHeating | heater | Loadpoint built on a heating device, the heating counterpart of a charging point. | Heizung |
| meter | meter | Device that reports power and energy at one point of the installation, e.g. grid, solar, battery or consumer. Inverters and batteries act as meters too. | Zähler |
| meterReading | meter reading | Cumulative energy counter of a meter in kWh, e.g. at session start and stop. | Zählerstand |
| meterUsage | usage | Role of a meter in the installation: grid, solar, battery, charger or consumer. | Verwendung |
| mileage | mileage | Odometer reading of a vehicle. | Kilometerstand |
| minimumCharge | minimum charge | Desired charge level that should be charged regardless of surplus or price. | Mindestladung |
| modeBoost | boost | Label of the Fast mode for continuous heating devices: request more power now. SG Ready mode 3. | Boost |
| modeFast | fast | Charge mode: full power now. Labelled Boost for continuous heating devices and On for switchable devices. | Schnell |
| modeNormal | normal | Label of the Off mode for continuous heating devices, which keep running on their own. SG Ready mode 2. | Normal |
| modeOff | off | Charge mode: no charging. Shown as Normal for continuous heating devices. | Aus |
| modeOn | on | Label of the Fast mode for switchable devices: switch on now. | Ein |
| modeSmart | smart | Charge mode: surplus plus cheap or clean grid energy, following plans and limits. Keep the English word in your language. Translate only if the English word would irritate users. | Smart |
| optimizer | optimizer | Battery and charging optimization feature. Keep the English word in your language. Translate only if the English word would irritate users. | Optimizer |
| page | page | Top-level view reachable from the tab bar or menu. | Seite |
| pageCharge | Charge page | The start page with charging points and energy flow, named after its tab bar label. | Laden-Seite |
| power | power | Instantaneous rate, in W or kW. Not energy. | Leistung |
| priceLimit | price limit | Maximum price per kWh at which smart mode charges from the grid. | Preislimit |
| production | production | Energy generated on site. Usually PV, deliberately open to other sources like wind. | Erzeugung |
| savings | savings | Money saved compared to charging everything from the grid at the reference price. | Ersparnis |
| selfConsumption | self-consumption | Share of solar production used on site instead of exported. | Eigenverbrauch |
| solar | solar | Energy from the PV system. Always solar in English, not PV or photovoltaic. | PV |
| solarSystem | solar system | The PV installation as a device. | PV-Anlage |
| sponsorToken | sponsor token | Token that unlocks sponsor-only features and devices. Two words, lowercase in body text. | Sponsortoken |
| summativeEnergyMeasurement | summative energy measurement | Meter nets signed power across phases each instant. Not phase balancing, not net metering. | Phasensaldierung |
| surplus | surplus | Production minus house consumption, available for charging or heating. | Überschuss |
| switchableSocket | switchable socket | Socket, plug or relay that is switched on and off, integrated as a charger. | schaltbare Steckdose |
| tariff | tariff | Price structure over time for grid import or grid export, static or dynamic. | Tarif |
| tariffSurcharge | surcharge | Fixed amount per kWh added to the energy price, e.g. grid fees or provider margin. Not the charge level. | Aufschlag |
| usage | usage | Energy overview view showing where energy went. | Verwendung |
| valueRange | range | Span between lowest and highest value in a forecast or chart, e.g. price or temperature range. | Bereich |
| vehicle | vehicle | An EV known to evcc, with its battery capacity and charge level. | Fahrzeug |
| vehicleLimit | vehicle limit | Charge limit set in the vehicle itself, not in evcc. | Fahrzeuglimit |
| vehicleRange | range | Distance a vehicle can drive, in km or mi. | Reichweite |
| withdrawal | withdrawal | Energy taken from a costly source: grid import and battery discharge. Counterpart of production in the energy chart, which groups energy used directly from own sources. Not a financial term. | Entnahme |
