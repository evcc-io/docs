| dynamicTariff | dynamic tariff | Tariff whose prices change over time, e.g. every hour or 15 minutes. | dynamisk tarif |

| externalLimit | external limit | Limit imposed by the grid operator or an external system. Code says HEMS for historical reasons; never use that acronym for this feature in UI text. | ekstern grænse |
| forecast | forecast | Solar, price or CO₂ forecast. | prognose |
| grid | grid | Public electricity grid. | elnet |

| gridImport | grid import | Energy taken from the grid. Short label import inside a grid card. | import fra elnettet |

| homeBattery | home battery | Stationary battery in the house, as opposed to the vehicle battery. | hjemmebatteri |
| loadpoint | charging point or heater | Developer term for the controllable unit on top of a device, i.e. a charging point or a heater. Never used in UI text. | ladepunkt eller varmeenhed |
| loadpointCharging | charging point | Loadpoint built on a charger, controls a vehicle. | ladepunkt |
| loadpointHeating | heater | Loadpoint built on a heating device, the heating counterpart of a charging point. | varmeenhed |
| meter | meter | Device that reports power and energy at one point of the installation, e.g. grid, solar, battery or consumer. Inverters and batteries act as meters too. | måler |
| meterReading | meter reading | Cumulative energy counter of a meter in kWh, e.g. at session start and stop. | målerstand |
| meterUsage | usage | Role of a meter in the installation: grid, solar, battery, charger or consumer. | anvendelse |
| mileage | mileage | Odometer reading of a vehicle. | kilometerstand |
| minimumCharge | minimum charge | Desired charge level that should be charged regardless of surplus or price. | minimumsopladning |
| modeBoost | boost | Label of the Fast mode for continuous heating devices: request more power now. SG Ready mode 3. | boost |
| modeFast | fast | Charge mode: full power now. Labelled Boost for continuous heating devices and On for switchable devices. | hurtig |
| modeNormal | normal | Label of the Off mode for continuous heating devices, which keep running on their own. SG Ready mode 2. | normal |
| modeOff | off | Charge mode: no charging. Shown as Normal for continuous heating devices. | fra |
| modeOn | on | Label of the Fast mode for switchable devices: switch on now. | aktiveret |
| modeSmart | smart | Charge mode: surplus plus cheap or clean grid energy, following plans and limits. Keep the English word in your language. Translate only if the English word would irritate users. | smart |
| optimizer | optimizer | Battery and charging optimization feature. Keep the English word in your language. Translate only if the English word would irritate users. | optimizer |
| page | page | Top-level view reachable from the tab bar or menu. | side |
| pageCharge | Charge page | The start page with charging points and energy flow, named after its tab bar label. | Opladning-siden |
| power | power | Instantaneous rate, in W or kW. Not energy. | effekt |
| priceLimit | price limit | Maximum price per kWh at which smart mode charges from the grid. | prisgrænse |
| production | production | Energy generated on site. Usually PV, deliberately open to other sources like wind. | produktion |
| savings | savings | Money saved compared to charging everything from the grid at the reference price. | besparelse |
| selfConsumption | self-consumption | Share of solar production used on site instead of exported. | egetforbrug |
| solar | solar | Energy from the PV system. Always solar in English, not PV or photovoltaic. | sol |
| solarSystem | solar system | The PV installation as a device. | solcelleanlæg |
| sponsorToken | sponsor token | Token that unlocks sponsor-only features and devices. Two words, lowercase in body text. | sponsortoken |
| summativeEnergyMeasurement | summative energy measurement | Meter nets signed power across phases each instant. Not phase balancing, not net metering. | summationsmåling |
| surplus | surplus | Production minus house consumption, available for charging or heating. | overskud |
| switchableSocket | switchable socket | Socket, plug or relay that is switched on and off, integrated as a charger. | styrbar stikkontakt |
| tariff | tariff | Price structure over time for grid import or grid export, static or dynamic. | tarif |
| tariffSurcharge | surcharge | Fixed amount per kWh added to the energy price, e.g. grid fees or provider margin. Not the charge level. | tillæg |
| usage | usage | Energy overview view showing where energy went. | anvendelse |
| valueRange | range | Span between lowest and highest value in a forecast or chart, e.g. price or temperature range. | spændvidde |
| vehicle | vehicle | An EV known to evcc, with its battery capacity and charge level. | køretøj |
| vehicleLimit | vehicle limit | Charge limit set in the vehicle itself, not in evcc. | køretøjets grænse |
| vehicleRange | range | Distance a vehicle can drive, in km or mi. | rækkevidde |
| withdrawal | withdrawal | Energy taken from a costly source: grid import and battery discharge. Counterpart of production in the energy chart, which groups energy used directly from own sources. Not a financial term. | aftag |
