## Glossary

Agreed term per concept. Base form; inflect as the grammar requires.

| key | en | description | it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| autarky | autarky | Share of consumption covered without the grid. | autosufficienza |
| authorization | authorization | Releasing a charging point for charging, e.g. by RFID or app. | autorizzazione |
| batteryBoost | battery boost | Smart mode setting: charge the vehicle from the home battery down to a limit. | boost da batteria |
| batteryCharge | battery charge | Energy flowing into the home battery. | carica della batteria |
| batteryDischarge | battery discharge | Energy flowing out of the home battery. | scarica della batteria |
| card | card | A boxed UI panel on a page. Not tile or panel. | scheda |
| charged | charged | Energy that went into the vehicle or battery during a session or period. | caricato |

| chargeLevelShort | charge | Short form of charge level for labels and table headers. | carica |
| charger | charger | Device level: the wallbox hardware. The charging point is the loadpoint using it. | caricabatterie |
| chargingPlan | charging plan | Target charge level by a point in time. Plural also names the feature and its dialog. | piano di ricarica |
| chargingSession | charging session | One plug-in to unplug cycle, as shown in the sessions log. | sessione di ricarica |
| chargingSessionShort | session | Short form of charging session for tight spaces such as the tab bar. | sessione |
| cheapEnergy | cheap energy | Grid energy below the price limit. | energia economica |
| circuit | circuit | Nestable load management group with a current or power limit that caps everything below it. | circuito |
| circuitRoot | root circuit | Top-level circuit of the nesting, usually the grid connection. All other circuits sit below it. | circuito principale |
| circuitSub | sub-circuit | Circuit nested inside another circuit. | sotto-circuito |
| cleanEnergy | clean energy | Grid energy below the CO₂ limit. | energia pulita |
| co2 | CO₂ | Carbon dioxide. Written with the Unicode subscript character ₂ in every language, not CO2. | CO₂ |
| co2Limit | CO₂ limit | Maximum grid CO₂ intensity at which smart mode charges from the grid. | limite CO₂ |
| consumer | consumer | A metered load that evcc does not control, part of house consumption. | consumatore |
| consumption | consumption | Energy used in the house excluding charging and heating controlled by evcc. | consumo |
| cost | cost | Total amount of money for a session or period. Price is per kWh, cost is the sum. | costo |
| curtailment | curtailment | Limiting PV production, e.g. the 60% or 70% rule. | limitazione della produzione |
| dialog | dialog | Overlay opened from a page for settings or confirmations. Called modal only in code. | finestra di dialogo |
| dynamicTariff | dynamic tariff | Tariff whose prices change over time, e.g. every hour or 15 minutes. | tariffa dinamica |
| emissions | emissions | CO₂ caused by charged energy, in kg or g/kWh. | emissioni |
| energy | energy | Amount over time, in Wh or kWh. Not power. | energia |
| energyFlow | energy flow | Live diagram of production, grid, battery and consumption. | flusso di energia |
| energyPrice | energy price | Price per kWh at a point in time. | prezzo dell'energia |
| evcc | evcc | Product name. Always lowercase in every language, also at sentence start. | evcc |
| externalLimit | external limit | Limit imposed by the grid operator or an external system. Code says HEMS for historical reasons; never use that acronym for this feature in UI text. | limite esterno |
| forecast | forecast | Solar, price or CO₂ forecast. | previsione |
| grid | grid | Public electricity grid. | rete |
| gridExport | grid export | Energy fed into the grid. Short label export inside a grid card. One term throughout, no synonyms such as feed-in. | immissione in rete |
| gridExportTariff | grid export tariff | Tariff for energy fed into the grid, i.e. the remuneration. Builds on the grid export term; one word for both is fine if the language has no distinction. | tariffa di immissione |
| gridImport | grid import | Energy taken from the grid. Short label import inside a grid card. | prelievo dalla rete |
| gridImportTariff | grid import tariff | Tariff for energy taken from the grid. | tariffa di prelievo |
| gridMeter | grid meter | Meter at the grid connection point. | contatore di rete |
| guestVehicle | guest vehicle | Unknown vehicle at a charging point, not configured in evcc. | veicolo ospite |
| heatingDevice | heating device | Device level: heat pump, heating rod etc. The heating counterpart of a charger. | dispositivo di riscaldamento |
| homeBattery | home battery | Stationary battery in the house, as opposed to the vehicle battery. | batteria domestica |
| loadpoint | charging point or heater | Developer term for the controllable unit on top of a device, i.e. a charging point or a heater. Never used in UI text. | punto di ricarica o riscaldatore |
| loadpointCharging | charging point | Loadpoint built on a charger, controls a vehicle. | punto di ricarica |
| loadpointHeating | heater | Loadpoint built on a heating device, the heating counterpart of a charging point. | riscaldatore |
| meter | meter | Device that reports power and energy at one point of the installation, e.g. grid, solar, battery or consumer. Inverters and batteries act as meters too. | contatore |
| meterReading | meter reading | Cumulative energy counter of a meter in kWh, e.g. at session start and stop. | lettura del contatore |
| meterUsage | usage | Role of a meter in the installation: grid, solar, battery, charger or consumer. | utilizzo |
| mileage | mileage | Odometer reading of a vehicle. | chilometraggio |
| minimumCharge | minimum charge | Desired charge level that should be charged regardless of surplus or price. | ricarica minima |
| modeBoost | boost | Label of the Fast mode for continuous heating devices: request more power now. SG Ready mode 3. | boost |
| modeFast | fast | Charge mode: full power now. Labelled Boost for continuous heating devices and On for switchable devices. | veloce |
| modeNormal | normal | Label of the Off mode for continuous heating devices, which keep running on their own. SG Ready mode 2. | normale |
| modeOff | off | Charge mode: no charging. Shown as Normal for continuous heating devices. | off |
| modeOn | on | Label of the Fast mode for switchable devices: switch on now. | on |
| modeSmart | smart | Charge mode: surplus plus cheap or clean grid energy, following plans and limits. Keep the English word in your language. Translate only if the English word would irritate users. | intelligente |
| optimizer | optimizer | Battery and charging optimization feature. Keep the English word in your language. Translate only if the English word would irritate users. | ottimizzatore |
| page | page | Top-level view reachable from the tab bar or menu. | pagina |
| pageCharge | Charge page | The start page with charging points and energy flow, named after its tab bar label. | pagina Carica |
| power | power | Instantaneous rate, in W or kW. Not energy. | potenza |
| priceLimit | price limit | Maximum price per kWh at which smart mode charges from the grid. | limite di prezzo |
| production | production | Energy generated on site. Usually PV, deliberately open to other sources like wind. | produzione |
| savings | savings | Money saved compared to charging everything from the grid at the reference price. | risparmio |
| selfConsumption | self-consumption | Share of solar production used on site instead of exported. | autoconsumo |
| solar | solar | Energy from the PV system. Always solar in English, not PV or photovoltaic. | solare |
| solarSystem | solar system | The PV installation as a device. | impianto fotovoltaico |
| sponsorToken | sponsor token | Token that unlocks sponsor-only features and devices. Two words, lowercase in body text. | token sponsor |
| summativeEnergyMeasurement | summative energy measurement | Meter nets signed power across phases each instant. Not phase balancing, not net metering. | misurazione a saldo tra le fasi |
| surplus | surplus | Production minus house consumption, available for charging or heating. | surplus |
| switchableSocket | switchable socket | Socket, plug or relay that is switched on and off, integrated as a charger. | presa commutabile |
| tariff | tariff | Price structure over time for grid import or grid export, static or dynamic. | tariffa |
| tariffSurcharge | surcharge | Fixed amount per kWh added to the energy price, e.g. grid fees or provider margin. Not the charge level. | maggiorazione |
| usage | usage | Energy overview view showing where energy went. | utilizzo |
| valueRange | range | Span between lowest and highest value in a forecast or chart, e.g. price or temperature range. | intervallo |
| vehicle | vehicle | An EV known to evcc, with its battery capacity and charge level. | veicolo |
| vehicleLimit | vehicle limit | Charge limit set in the vehicle itself, not in evcc. | limite del veicolo |
| vehicleRange | range | Distance a vehicle can drive, in km or mi. | autonomia |
| withdrawal | withdrawal | Energy taken from a costly source: grid import and battery discharge. Counterpart of production in the energy chart, which groups energy used directly from own sources. Not a financial term. | prelievo |
