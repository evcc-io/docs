# Style Sheet: Danish (da)

- Address the reader with "du", "din", "dit" and "dine", lowercase, consistent with the evcc UI.
- Use the imperative for steps the reader performs ("Åbn **Opsætning**") and impersonal phrasing for technical requirements ("Måleren skal levere effekten").
- Headings use sentence case: only the first word and proper names are capitalised.
- Highlight blog posts are titled "Højdepunkter: …" (not "Highlights"), lowercase after the colon.
- Write compounds closed as one word (ladepunkt, hjemmebatteri, solcelleanlæg, elnetsmåler, ladeplan).
- Use a hyphen after abbreviations and symbols: CO₂-grænse, OCPP-forbindelse, MQTT-broker, evcc-installation.
- Numbers: decimal comma and thousands period (1,4 kW, 11.200 W), a space between number and unit and before percent (50 %).
- Quotation marks ”…” for actual quotations, italics rather than quotes for technical terms.
- Abbreviations: f.eks., dvs., osv., inkl.
- Dates in prose: "18. februar 2026", months and weekdays lowercase; times 14.30.
- Mode names come from the UI: **Smart**, **Hurtig**, **Fra**, **Aktiveret**, **Boost**, **Min+Sol**, **Sol**.
- Refer to a running instance as "din evcc-installation"; "evcc" stays lowercase, also at the start of a sentence.
- Prefer Danish words where they are established (indstillinger, notifikationer, ladeeffekt); keep English only for UI strings that the UI itself leaves in English.
- Avoid English word order and split compounds ("lade punkt" is wrong, "ladepunkt" is right).
- Keep the tone direct and friendly, no marketing words (nemt, problemfrit).
