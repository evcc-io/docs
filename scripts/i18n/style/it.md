# Style Sheet: Italian (it)

- Address the reader with "tu", lowercase, consistent with the evcc UI.
- Use the imperative for steps the reader performs ("Apri **Configurazione**") and impersonal phrasing for technical requirements ("Il contatore deve fornire la potenza").
- Headings and labels use sentence case; capitals only for CO₂, proper names, product names and page titles.
- Use accented vowels correctly (perché, è, È, più) and elision with the typographic apostrophe ’ (l’impianto, un’auto).
- Numbers: decimal comma and thousands period (1,4 kW, 11.200 W), a space between number and unit, no space before percent (50%).
- Quotation marks “…” for actual quotations, italics rather than quotes for technical terms.
- Abbreviations: ad es., cioè, ecc., incl.
- Dates in prose: "18 febbraio 2026", months and weekdays lowercase; times 14:30.
- Mode names come from the UI: **Intelligente**, **Veloce**, **Off**, **On**, **Boost**, **Min+Solare**, **Solare**.
- Refer to a running instance as "la tua istanza evcc"; "evcc" stays lowercase, also at the start of a sentence.
- Prefer Italian words where they are established (impostazioni, notifiche, ricarica, inverter is accepted); keep English only for UI strings that the UI itself leaves in English.
- Avoid English word order, needless passive voice and calques such as "fare uso di" for "usare".
- Keep the tone direct and friendly, no marketing words (comodamente, senza soluzione di continuità).
