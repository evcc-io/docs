# Style Sheet: Dutch (nl)

- Address the reader formally with "u" and "uw", consistent with the evcc UI; never "je", "jij" or "jouw".
- Use the imperative for steps the reader performs ("Open **Configuratie**") and impersonal phrasing for technical requirements ("De meter moet het vermogen leveren").
- Headings use sentence case: only the first word and proper names are capitalised.
- Write compounds closed as one word (laadpunt, thuisbatterij, netmeter, laadsessie, gastvoertuig).
- Use a hyphen after symbols, abbreviations and acronyms: CO₂-limiet, OCPP-verbinding, MQTT-broker, evcc-installatie.
- Numbers: decimal comma and thousands period (1,4 kW, 11.200 W), a space between number and unit, no space before percent (50%).
- Quotation marks “…” for actual quotations, the typographic apostrophe ’ (auto’s), italics rather than quotes for technical terms.
- Abbreviations: bijv., d.w.z., enz., incl.; write them out at the start of a sentence.
- Dates in prose: "18 februari 2026", months and weekdays lowercase; times 14:30.
- Mode names come from the UI: **Slim**, **Snel**, **Uit**, **Aan**, **Boost**, **Min+PV**, **PV**.
- Refer to a running instance as "uw evcc-installatie"; "evcc" stays lowercase, also at the start of a sentence.
- Prefer Dutch words where they are established (instellingen, meldingen, laadvermogen); keep English only for UI strings that the UI itself leaves in English.
- Avoid English word order and calques such as "maakt gebruik van" for "uses" when "gebruikt" is enough.
- Keep the docs' informal, direct tone despite the formal "u": short sentences, no marketing words (gemakkelijk, naadloos).
