# Style Sheet: English (en)

English is the source language: these rules bind the hand-written pages under `src/content/docs/en/` (the full writing guide is in `AGENTS.md`) and tell translators what the source does on purpose.

- Address the reader with "you"; use the imperative for steps the reader performs ("Open **Configuration**") and an impersonal subject for technical requirements ("The plugin must return the meter reading").
- Headings use title case ("Frequently Asked Questions"); the other locales follow their own sheets.
- UK spelling (optimise, behaviour, colour); the exception is the **optimizer** feature, spelled with "z" like the UI, while generic verbs and nouns keep UK spelling (optimising, optimisation).
- Say "solar" rather than "PV" or "photovoltaic"; "sponsor token" is two words, lowercase in body text and title case only in headings.
- Numbers: decimal point and thousands comma (1.4 kW, 11,200 W), a space between number and unit, no space before percent (50%), a space after the paragraph sign in body text (§ 14a EnWG).
- Quotation marks "…" for actual quotations, italics rather than quotes for technical terms.
- Abbreviations: e.g., i.e., etc.; never "for example" where "e.g." does.
- Dates in prose: "18 February 2026"; times 14:30.
- Mode names come from the UI: **Smart**, **Fast**, **Off**, **On**, **Boost**.
- Refer to a running instance as "your evcc instance"; "evcc" stays lowercase, also at the start of a sentence, and is mentioned only when the context needs it.
- Use the glossary terms (`../evcc/i18n/glossary/en.json`): charging point, not loadpoint; charger for the wallbox hardware; home battery; charge level, never SoC; card for a boxed UI panel.
- Keep the tone direct and friendly: complete sentences, no marketing words (convenient, seamlessly), no em or en dashes as sentence connectors.
