Curtailment acts on the inverters.
Most inverters are curtailed through their solar meter configuration.
They are marked **Curtailable** on the [PV, Battery, Grid, Meter](/en/meters) page and need no additional setup.

If there are several inverters or a dedicated feed-in limiter such as the SMA Sunny Home Manager 2.0, set up a separate **curtailment device**.
The same applies when the inverter can't be curtailed through its meter configuration.
With several inverters, the limit has to be written to the leader inverter (e.g. SolarEdge).
The curtailment signal is passed as a percentage to all curtailable meters and curtailment devices alike.
Each device applies it relative to its own nominal power.
The **Installed generator power** of the curtailment device therefore has to cover the whole installation.

Curtailment devices are set up via **Configuration → Grid → Add curtailment device**.
The button appears once a solar meter is configured.
A curtailment device only becomes active when one of the [integrations](#integrations) above signals a feed-in limit.
