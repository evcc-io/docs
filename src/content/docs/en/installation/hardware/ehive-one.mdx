The eHive One is a compact computer for the DIN rail on which evcc runs as an app.
It is powered by 12 to 30 V DC or Power over Ethernet, and a built-in UPS bridges short power cuts.

## Extras \{#extras\}

<NavCardGrid>

<NavCard title="Installer access">

Installers can get time-limited, password-protected access to the web UIs of all apps from outside.

</NavCard>

<NavCard title="UPS">

The built-in UPS keeps the device running through short power cuts.

</NavCard>

<NavCard title="Backups">

Updates can be rolled back, and backups can be written to a USB drive.

</NavCard>

<NavCard title="Low power consumption">

The device draws about 3 W in typical operation.

</NavCard>

<NavCard title="Additional features">

The device's own web UI, called SmartHub, installs and updates apps.
Besides evcc there are apps for billing charging sessions and tenants, for monitoring and for a KNX bridge.

</NavCard>

</NavCardGrid>
