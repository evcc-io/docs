The EnergyLink One from SPiNE is a gateway for the meter cabinet on which evcc runs as an app.
The integrated energy meter can take the place of a separate meter at the grid connection point.
The device is sold through installers.

## Extras \{#extras\}

<NavCardGrid>

<NavCard title="Integrated energy meter">

The built-in meter is a high-quality meter that can be used e.g. as the grid meter.

</NavCard>

evcc is one of several energy apps that are installed from the EnergyLink Portal.

</NavCard>

An emulator of the grid operator's control box lets the installer test the setup for § 14a EnWG and § 9 EEG.

## Getting Started \{#setup\}

- An electrician mounts the EnergyLink One on the DIN rail and connects Ethernet.
- Install evcc as an app in the [EnergyLink Portal](https://energylink.cloud).
- Open `http://EM400-XXXXXXXX:7070` with the device ID shown in the portal and start your evcc configuration.
