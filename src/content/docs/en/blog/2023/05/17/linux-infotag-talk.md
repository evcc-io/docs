At the end of April, after a long break due to the coronavirus pandemic, the [19th Linux Infotag](https://www.luga.de/static/LIT-2023/) took place. The Infotag is an open source community event organised by the [Linux User Group Augsburg e.V. (LUGA)](https://www.luga.de/).

<!-- excerpt -->

## Video Recording Is Now Available

Among many other exciting topics, we had the opportunity to present our project this year. The video recording is now available too.

[![YouTube screenshot](./youtube.webp)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qN8JwBWOlzw)

[YouTube: Open Source Sonne tanken - Wallboxen mit evcc smarter machen (de)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qN8JwBWOlzw)

Besides a general introduction to charging electric cars with your own solar power, the talk covered smart grid charging, and I gave some insights into our development process and our funding model. Feel free to take a look.

You can find the slides for the talk on [Speaker Deck](https://speakerdeck.com/naltatis/open-source-sonne-tanken-wallboxen-mit-evcc-smarter-machen). 👇
