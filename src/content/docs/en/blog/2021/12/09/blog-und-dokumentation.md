evcc now has documentation and this blog, and today we'd like to introduce both of them in a bit more detail.

## A Few Numbers

On 6 March 2020 [andig](https://github.com/andig/) brought **evcc** to life with the [first commit](https://github.com/evcc-io/evcc/commit/3c503b333dfc9a1206dd8bcfbfda89d93746c2c6).
He could never have dreamed of the path this project has taken since then.
More than 1,300 commits later, with [369 stars](https://github.com/evcc-io/evcc/stargazers), [90 forks](https://github.com/evcc-io/evcc/network/members), [34 contributors](https://github.com/evcc-io/evcc/graphs/contributors) and more than [70 releases](https://github.com/evcc-io/evcc/releases), **evcc** keeps on growing.

<!-- excerpt -->

The number of users is growing too, even though we don't have exact figures (and we don't need them).
The [forum](https://github.com/evcc-io/evcc/discussions) already counts almost 450 discussions with many participants.
Lots of interested people also hang out in our [developer Slack channel](https://evcc.io/slack).

## New Documentation

To give this broad range of people, especially with their different technical backgrounds, better access to **evcc**, we have started building new documentation at [docs.evcc.io](https://docs.evcc.io/docs/Home) based on the open source project [Docusaurus](https://docusaurus.io).

We have set up the following sections:

- **Getting Started**: installation and configuration of **evcc**.
- **Guides**: various topics and frequently asked questions.
- **Devices**: a list of known, supported devices and their configuration.
- **Reference**: all the (including technical) details and configuration options for those who want to dig a little deeper.

We'd be happy if you supported us with this.
Whether it's fixing typos or adding new and better content, the documentation is also on GitHub at [github.com/evcc-io/docs](https://github.com/evcc-io/docs) and we look forward to your contributions!

## Blog

We're also launching this blog today.
Every now and then you'll find various content about **evcc** and topics around charging at home here.
We have a few ideas, so stay tuned.
Our main focus remains on developing **evcc** itself, though.
But a look behind the scenes is surely interesting for some of you every now and then :)
